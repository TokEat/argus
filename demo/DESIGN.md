# Argus Demo Video — Design

## Style Prompt
Data Drift aesthetic — futuristic, immersive, otherworldly. Deep black canvas with electric purple and cyan accents. Thin futuristic type, minimal text. Grid dissolve transitions between scenes. Particles and glowing nodes suggest distributed systems and data flow.

## Colors
- Background: `#0a0a0a` (deep black)
- Surface: `#141420` (dark panel)
- Accent Primary: `#7c3aed` (electric purple)
- Accent Secondary: `#06b6d4` (cyan)
- Text Primary: `#e8e8f0` (near-white)
- Text Secondary: `#8888a8` (muted)
- Success/Online: `#22c55e` (green)
- Danger/Vuln: `#ef4444` (red)

## Typography
- Headlines: Space Grotesk (700-900 weight)
- Body: Space Grotesk (300-400 weight)
- Code/Data: JetBrains Mono

## What NOT to Do
- No gradient text (background-clip hack)
- No cyan-on-dark generic neon — use purple as primary, cyan as secondary only
- No centered equal-weight layouts — lead the eye with hierarchy
- No Inter/Roboto/Poppins
- No `<br>` in content text
